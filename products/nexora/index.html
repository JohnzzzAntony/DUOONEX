Nexora help desk & work management

A shared workspace for support tickets, tasks, approvals and customer service, with organisation-scoped access and activity history.

Type
NexPixels-owned product
Category
Help desk & work management
Technology
Next.js

The brief

Nexora is a help desk and work management application for teams handling service requests alongside everyday tasks. Staff can assign tickets, track status, add internal notes and coordinate project work. Customers have a separate portal, while organisation-level permissions keep each workspace focused on its own records.

Approach

  1. Route incoming requests to the right owner and preserve the conversation history.
  2. Keep task checklists, assignees and tracked time beside project work.
  3. Give customers a dedicated place to follow their support requests.
  4. Control workspace settings, permissions, notifications and knowledge content.

Experience

A request becomes an assigned ticket with a visible status and activity trail. Agents can reply to customers, keep internal context private and link operational work to tasks. Dashboards bring outstanding requests into view so teams can decide what needs attention next.

  • Ticket assignment and status transitions
  • Public replies and private internal notes
  • Project tasks, checklists and time tracking
  • Customer portal and knowledge articles
  • SLA targets, assets, departments and locations
  • Workspace roles, API keys and session management

Development

Tenant-scoped queries and role checks support separate workspaces. Ticket transitions, attachment access and approval actions follow server-side rules. The interface brings these rules into the daily workflow through status labels, assignment controls and a consistent navigation structure.

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Prisma and PostgreSQL, the application connects ticket handling with assets, knowledge articles and approvals. Its source includes authentication, workspace membership, private attachments and scoped API access. The device mockups show the seeded demonstration workspace (fictional users and requests), not a customer account.

Scope and outcome

Developed application; not deployed to a public live URL. The implemented scope covers support operations, team tasks and workspace administration. Advanced automation editors, billing checkout and live collaboration are outside the documented completed scope; integrations require their own configuration.

Nexora demonstrates how a custom web application can connect service delivery with internal operations. For a similar UAE platform, define staff roles, customer access, ticket states and reporting needs before choosing integrations.

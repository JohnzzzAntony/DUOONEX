NexPixels company and service information.

NexPixels is a Dubai-based agency building websites, online stores and custom business applications for UAE, GCC and international clients. This page summarises its services and contact details.

Company

NexPixels is a Dubai-based web development agency serving businesses in the UAE, the wider GCC and international markets. Its services cover custom websites, web applications, Shopify and WordPress development, UI/UX design in Figma, and Python-backed integrations. Projects start with business requirements, content and user journeys, then move through design, implementation, testing and handover. Pricing and support are agreed around each project’s scope.

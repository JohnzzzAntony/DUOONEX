# Employment work in the NexPixels portfolio

The eight e-commerce entries are labelled **Design · development · launch / employment work** on the Products and Case Studies inner pages. The introduction uses the supplied role: **Designed, built and shipped complete e-commerce platforms as an employee.** Each entry includes its name, category, actual homepage capture and live link, without a detailed case study. The employer is not named because no employer name was supplied.

Before public release, obtain written permission from the employer and any relevant client/rightsholder for portfolio display, including screenshots, brand names and logos. Check the employment contract, NDA, client terms and applicable jurisdiction. A disclosure gives accurate attribution; it does not itself grant permission to reproduce protected work.

Suggested permission request (not sent):

> May I include the following publicly accessible websites in the professional-experience section of the NexPixels website: Karji, Atelier Perfumery, Daima, Touch of Oud, Saleel Parfums, Juvenis Paris, Flower District and The Exceptional Parfums? The section will identify my contribution during employment with [employer], describe only my actual role [role], and credit the employer and relevant rights holders using the wording you approve. Please confirm permission to use the website names, links and homepage screenshots for this purpose, and any client approval, exclusions, duration or removal requirements.

Once permission is available, add the approved employer name and precise contribution to each entry. If permission is declined or an NDA prevents identification, replace those entries with anonymised descriptions of your experience and independently created demonstrations that contain no employer/client assets or confidential information.

For UAE federal employment-related work, Article 28 of Federal Decree-Law No. 38 of 2021 generally attributes economic rights to the employer in the specified employment circumstances, unless otherwise agreed in writing. Contracts and other applicable jurisdictions can affect the position. This is practical portfolio guidance, not a determination of the user's legal rights.

Source: https://uaelegislation.gov.ae/en/legislations/1534/download

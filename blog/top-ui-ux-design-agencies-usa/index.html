How to Compare UI/UX Design Agencies for a UAE Project

NP NexPixels editorial · Updated 12 October 2026
UI UX

Assess a UI/UX partner by the decisions behind the work, not a self-published agency ranking. For a UAE project, look for relevant workflows, clear deliverables and a practical review process. Location matters less than whether collaboration and implementation responsibilities are understood.

Evaluate the deliverables

Ask how user evidence changed a journey or interface decision. Review responsive screens, forms, empty states and error feedback alongside the final visuals. A design system should explain reusable behaviour and support development rather than simply display a set of components.

Practical review checklist

  • Look for relevant e-commerce or business-application work.
  • Ask how the team validates assumptions about users.
  • Confirm what developers receive at handover.
  • Review feedback rounds, dependencies and scope-change terms.

Clarify what you need designed

UI/UX work ranges from a quick audit of an existing site to research, information architecture, wireframes, high-fidelity interfaces, prototypes and a full design system. Before contacting agencies, decide which of these you need and whether development is included. Sharing the same brief with every agency makes proposals much easier to compare.

Review case studies critically

Look beyond the final screens. A useful case study explains the original problem, who the users were, what research was done, which options were explored and why the final direction was chosen. Be cautious of case studies that only show visuals or claim large results without explaining how they were measured.

Ask about research and testing

Good agencies validate designs with real users. Ask how they recruit participants, whether they test in Arabic and English where relevant, and how findings change the design. Even a small number of usability sessions can reveal problems that internal reviews miss.

  • What will we receive at each stage, and in which format?
  • How many rounds of revisions are included?
  • Will the Figma files be organised with components for developers?
  • How do you support developers during implementation?
  • How do you handle accessibility and right-to-left layouts?

Local versus international agencies

International agencies can bring broad experience, while a UAE-based team understands local payment habits, bilingual content and business culture, and can join in-person workshops. Whichever you choose, check time-zone overlap, communication style and how decisions will be documented.

Evaluate the handover

The value of design depends on how well it is implemented. Ask to see an example handover file: components, spacing tokens, interaction notes and responsive behaviour. Clear handover reduces back-and-forth with developers and keeps the final product close to the approved design.

Share a consistent project brief

Share the audience, core tasks, content and technical constraints before requesting a proposal. Agree the Figma deliverables, review rounds and developer handover.

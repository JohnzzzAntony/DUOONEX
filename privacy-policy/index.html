Privacy policy for the NexPixels website.

Information about contacting NexPixels, the details you choose to share and how to ask a question about your personal information.

Information you choose to share

When you use our enquiry form, email or WhatsApp, you may share your name, contact details, budget and project requirements. These details help us respond to your request. Do not include passwords, payment card details or sensitive records in an initial enquiry.

Enquiry correspondence

NexPixels uses project information to understand your request, discuss requirements and prepare a proposal. Any additional handling needed for a project should be described in the relevant agreement.

Website and external services

This website uses externally hosted fonts and links to email and WhatsApp. The enquiry form sends your submitted details to the NexPixels website inbox, where authorised administrators can review them. External providers process connection or account information under their own terms.

Project records

Please contact NexPixels to discuss which enquiry or project records are held and any applicable retention requirements. Specific project data-handling arrangements belong in the service agreement.

Privacy enquiries

For questions about personal information, or to request access, correction or deletion, email info@nexpixels.com. Requests are considered in the context of the information held and applicable obligations.

Email and WhatsApp

Using an email or WhatsApp link opens a third-party service. Review the information in your message before sending it and consult the provider’s privacy information for its handling practices.

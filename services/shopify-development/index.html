What your Shopify proposal should cover

Deliverables and ownership

Agree the theme approach, page templates, Liquid sections, catalogue structure, integrations and acceptance checks. Your proposal should identify who supplies product content, approves designs and owns the Shopify account.

Platform and app costs

Project fees cover the agreed delivery scope. Shopify subscriptions, paid themes, apps, payment processing and ongoing support are separate costs. We confirm required services and responsibilities before implementation; no unapproved fee or launch-date promise is included.

Payments for UAE merchants

Provider availability depends on your business, account and plan. Confirm eligibility in your Shopify admin before selecting a gateway. Test payment authorisation, failures, refunds and delivery rules against the supported configuration.

Launch and migration checks

Review catalogue completeness, redirects, metadata, domain configuration, notification emails and purchase paths. Keep a rollback plan and define who reviews the store after release.

Shopify Development questions

Shopify themes can support custom Liquid sections, product information, collection layouts and app integrations. The available payment and shipping options depend on your provider accounts and store configuration. We confirm those dependencies and test the mobile purchase journey as part of the agreed scope.

Custom Liquid sections can support product information, collection layouts and reusable content blocks. We define what store editors should control, then implement the agreed theme behaviour. App requirements and any theme limitations are checked before development.

Available integrations depend on the provider, merchant account and Shopify configuration. We review your selected payment and delivery services, confirm their supported connection methods and test the agreed buying journey. Provider subscriptions, account approvals and transaction fees remain separate dependencies.

We can assess the current platform before recommending a shopify development scope. Existing content, integrations and useful workflows are considered during planning.

Shopify Development is quoted around the agreed deliverables, complexity and dependencies. There is no single price that fits every UAE business.

Handover for shopify development documents the delivered scope and the responsibilities for maintaining it. Support is agreed around the platform and business needs.

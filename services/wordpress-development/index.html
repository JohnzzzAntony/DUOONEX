WordPress Development questions

Page templates, content volume, custom features, integrations and migration all affect the cost. A service website has different requirements from a store or membership platform. NexPixels prepares a scope-based proposal and identifies hosting, licensed plugins and ongoing support separately.

WordPress templates and reusable content fields can let your team update the agreed page content without editing code. We plan the publishing workflow, roles and page components first. Handover covers the editor controls and the responsibilities for ongoing updates.

Plugins are assessed against the required functionality, support and maintenance needs. We avoid adding a plugin where a simpler implementation meets the brief. Hosting, backups, plugin licences and update responsibilities are identified in the proposal rather than assumed to be included indefinitely.

We can assess the current platform before recommending a wordpress development scope. Existing content, integrations and useful workflows are considered during planning.

WordPress Development is quoted around the agreed deliverables, complexity and dependencies. There is no single price that fits every UAE business.

Handover for wordpress development documents the delivered scope and the responsibilities for maintaining it. Support is agreed around the platform and business needs.

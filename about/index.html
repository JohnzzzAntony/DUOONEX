A Dubai web development agency built around practical delivery

NexPixels brings design, e-commerce and software development together. We start with the business requirements, plan the user journey and choose a platform that fits the work.

Our capabilities span Shopify and Liquid, WordPress, React and Next.js, Python and Django, REST APIs and PostgreSQL. The stack follows the product requirements.

From a storefront to an internal dashboard, our focus is a usable interface, clear content, reliable integrations and testing before launch.

We define milestones, review points and handover responsibilities so the project remains understandable from brief to production.

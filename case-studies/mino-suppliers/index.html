/ Project overview

Mino Suppliers combines a marketing website with a trade portal. Visitors browse sustainable tissue, towel and napkin products and apply for a wholesale account. Approved business customers sign in to order at their own pricing tier, reuse order guides, route large orders for approval and follow invoices on net terms.

Built with Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma and PostgreSQL. Pricing tiers and customer-specific prices are resolved on the server, and orders above a company's approval threshold wait for an approver. The mockups use fictional customers and demonstration orders.

/ The customer journey

How the workflow works

A prospective customer reads about the products, submits a wholesale enquiry and is approved by the team. Purchasers then order from the portal at their agreed prices, approvers review orders above the limit, and finance follows invoices — while admins see leads, customers, orders and revenue in one place.

  • Product catalogue with certifications and recycled content
  • Wholesale application and lead management
  • Customer-specific pricing tiers and discounts
  • Order guides, cart and net-terms checkout

/ Design and development focus

Design and development focus

Roles distinguish visitors, purchasers, approvers, company owners and staff. Prices, approval rules and payment terms come from the customer's company record, so the same catalogue shows the correct commercial terms for each account.

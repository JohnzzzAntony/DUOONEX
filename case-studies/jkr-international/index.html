/ Project overview

JKR International supplies wheelchairs, patient-handling equipment, respiratory and diagnostic devices to hospitals, clinics and home-care customers. The platform supports both retail purchases and B2B quotation requests, with category, brand and collection browsing, product comparison, wishlists and order tracking.

The original platform was built with Django, PostgreSQL and Cloudinary; this Next.js and Prisma rebuild adds an enquiry cart, English and Arabic interface support and a custom admin panel. Product imagery in the mockups is illustrative placeholder artwork from the local demonstration build, not the client's production photography.

/ The customer journey

How the workflow works

Clinical buyers search or browse by category and brand, compare products and either add them to the enquiry cart for a quotation or purchase directly. The team manages orders, enquiries, content and catalogue updates from the admin panel.

  • Category, brand and collection catalogue
  • Enquiry cart for quotations alongside checkout
  • Product comparison, wishlist and recently viewed
  • English and Arabic interface support

/ Design and development focus

Design and development focus

Catalogue, orders, enquiries and content share one data model, so admin changes appear on the storefront immediately. Payment, transactional email and media storage are integrated through configurable providers.

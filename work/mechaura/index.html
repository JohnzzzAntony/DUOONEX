Mechaura International industrial supplier website

An industrial catalogue website connecting UAE buyers with product specifications, sector information and quotation enquiries.

Type
Client project
Category
Industrial supplier website
Technology
Vite
Website
mechaurainternational.com

The brief

Mechaura International is an industrial supplier website organised around products, sectors and quotation enquiries. Its catalogue covers abrasive brushes, bearings, hydraulic hoses and pumps, filtration, cutting tools and elevator accessories. Product imagery and motion content help buyers understand the range before contacting the supplier.

Approach

  1. Organise the catalogue around equipment categories and buyer terminology.
  2. Explain product applications through dedicated pages and original visuals.
  3. Connect industry and city content with relevant products and enquiry actions.
  4. Generate static pages, optimise assets and publish a crawlable sitemap.

Experience

Buyers can enter through search, a product category, an industry or a city page, review relevant equipment and move to a quotation enquiry. The website separates product information from company and service content so technical and commercial visitors can find an appropriate next step.

  • Product-led catalogue navigation and part-number search
  • Dedicated product family pages with specifications
  • Sector and Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah landing pages
  • Multi-step quotation and WhatsApp enquiry journeys
  • Technical guides for maintenance teams
  • Optimised WebP imagery, structured data and sitemap

Development

Product and editorial pages are generated as static content for delivery through the website build. Images are prepared in WebP, while videos provide supporting product context. Metadata, structured data and sitemap generation keep discovery tied to the pages that the build actually publishes.

The project uses a Vite build with generated product, sector, location and article pages, a sitemap and llms.txt workflow and optimised image assets. A companion WordPress theme packages the same design for WordPress hosting. The device mockups are captured from the production build of the website.

Scope and outcome

The delivered website scope is catalogue presentation and business enquiries. It is not presented as an online checkout or stock-control application. Product availability, quotation responses and fulfilment remain supplier-operated processes.

Mechaura shows how an industrial website can organise technical products for UAE and GCC buyers. A similar catalogue starts with product categories, usable specifications, sector requirements and a clear enquiry route.
